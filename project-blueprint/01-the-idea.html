The Idea

The one-paragraph brief this entire design is built from — nothing here that wasn't asked for, nothing implied that isn't included.

“Build an app that can be used to create a family tree with 10 generations deep at a minimum.”

Why this sentence drives everything

Two phrases in it decide every component that follows: “app…used to create” means a human builds the tree by hand, across more than one sitting. And “10 generations deep at a minimum” is the requirement that outranks everything else — a family tree tool that looks fine at 3 generations and breaks at 10 hasn’t done its job. See Components for the two pieces built specifically to guarantee that number holds.

Nothing in the paragraph mentions other people, other devices, imported records, or AI — so none of those appear in the design. A padded diagram would be a worse answer than a small one.

One paragraph becomes a small closed loop of four components, which together produce one thing: a tree that stays usable ten-plus generations deep.
