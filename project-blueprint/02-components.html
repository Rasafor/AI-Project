Components

Four real, idea-specific pieces — each one traceable back to specific words in the brief. No login system, no server, no external data source, and no AI layer, because the idea didn't ask for shared access, imported records, or anything generated/ranked by meaning.

Three layers, four boxes: one for the person to touch, two that guarantee the ten-generation requirement (marked ★), one to remember what was built.

The two-part depth guarantee

“10 generations deep at a minimum” is satisfied by two components working together, not one: the Genealogy Engine guarantees the depth can structurally exist (an open-ended relationship graph, not a form with 10 pre-printed slots), and the Tree Rendering Engine guarantees that depth can actually be seen and navigated once it does. If either is missing, the requirement isn't met — one without the other is either an unusable spreadsheet or a pretty renderer with nothing real to draw.
